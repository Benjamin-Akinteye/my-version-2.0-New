Assignment 2: What are the top 3 objective truths you discovered through experimentation and results?
Definition:
Objective truths do not depend on opinions. They hold true regardless of how people feel.

Truth #1: Starting preparation early reduces pressure and increases confidence.
 Evidence from my life: Whenever I begin studying or preparing for an exam or presentation well ahead of time, I feel calmer and more in control. I am able to revise properly, anticipate challenges, and perform better. In contrast, late preparation always leads to stress and self-doubt.

Truth #2: Repeated review leads to better long-term memory than forced memorization.
 Evidence from my life: When I consistently read through my notes multiple times over several days, the information sticks naturally without stress. However, when I try to cram or memorize in one sitting, I forget most of it shortly after. Repetition has proven more effective for me.

Truth #3: Networking significantly increases opportunities beyond what hard work alone can achieve.
 Evidence from my life: I have noticed that opportunities such as recommendations, collaborations, or access to information often come through people I know. Even when I worked hard in isolation, progress was slower. Building relationships opened doors that effort alone could not.
